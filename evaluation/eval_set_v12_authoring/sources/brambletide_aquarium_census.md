# Chapter 1 The Observation Desk

At 16:45 on 6 April, Adera Flint remained inside Quill Gallery at Brambletide Aquarium, entering observations beside its large viewing pane. She had not left that room during the count, and the desk stood well inside its doorway.

# Chapter 2 The Gallery Plan

At 16:45 on 6 April, Quill Gallery lay entirely on the lower floor of Brambletide Aquarium. The roof deck was a separate, nonoverlapping area two floors above it. No part of Quill Gallery extended onto the roof deck.

# Chapter 3 The Net Handover

At 15:50 on 6 April, the net named Cress was in Pellin Ward's sole physical custody. Liora Fenn was not holding or carrying it. Pellin secured its handle to his belt while Liora sorted the dry towels beside the preparation sink.

# Chapter 4 Labels on the Buckets

The gobies Marlo and Tivin arrived in separate buckets, but both name labels came loose before transfer. One fish entered tank S8 and the other tank S9. Neither the transfer notes nor their visible markings established which fish was Marlo.

# Chapter 5 A Shipment Without Follow-up

Four young eels left Brambletide alive in a transport box bound for Kelver Reach. The departure check recorded their condition, but the aquarium received neither an arrival report nor later observations of the animals at the destination.

# Chapter 6 Towels Along the Rail

At 17:10 on 6 April, Liora Fenn hung six blue towels along the preparation-room rail. She spaced them apart so their wet edges did not touch, then moved the empty laundry basket away from the doorway before the next trolley arrived.

# Chapter 7 The Visitor Bench

At 14:20 on 6 April, Pellin Ward moved the visitor bench away from a patch of dripping ceiling. He placed a plain bucket beneath the drip and pointed arriving visitors toward the dry seats, leaving the emergency walkway unobstructed.

# Chapter 8 After the Count

Adera closed the observation notebook after the last visitor left. The loose fish labels remained in an envelope marked for investigation, and the unanswered transport enquiry lay beside it. She kept both items apart from the completed census sheets.
