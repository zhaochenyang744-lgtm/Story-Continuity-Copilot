# Chapter 1 The Fiber Inspection

At 12:40 on 9 February, the teal sash marked H6 was made entirely of cotton, with no linen fibers or linen trim. Inspector Sorrel Venn attached the finding to its rental record after an earlier customer had guessed its material from its sheen.

# Chapter 2 Brown Paper

At 12:40 on 9 February, the silver cape was among the returned pieces wrapped in brown paper. Its rental tag remained attached outside the wrapping, where the intake clerk could read it without unfolding the long fabric.

# Chapter 3 The Sorting Containers

At 12:40 on 9 February, every brown-paper-wrapped returned piece at Mosswick lay inside the blue hamper. The yellow trunk was a separate, nonoverlapping container across the aisle. Neither container sat inside the other.

# Chapter 4 Under the Cutting Table

At 13:15 on 9 February, Belden Orr swept loose rivets from beneath the cutting table into a shallow pan. He left a missing-button notice on the counter, hoping the next delivery would include the correct replacements.

# Chapter 5 A Combined Refund

A receipt records a forty-crown refund to renter Neris Tal. The amount combines a deposit refund and a discount on rental fees, but gives neither component separately. Her original deposit was thirty crowns; the account breakdown is missing.

# Chapter 6 Samples from a Crate

Eight unlabelled mantles arrived in delivery crate K8. The clerk measured two and found matching lengths, but left the other six folded. No packing list specified their sizes, and the supplier had previously shipped both uniform and mixed-size sets.

# Chapter 7 The Window Display

At 16:10 on 9 February, Sorrel Venn replaced the faded window price card with a handwritten one. She placed a small ceramic bird against its lower edge to keep it upright, then stepped outside to check whether the lettering was readable.

# Chapter 8 Before Closing

The final customer paused beneath the striped awning while Belden gathered the empty hangers. Neris had left a request for her account breakdown. The clerk clipped it beside the refund receipt so it would be visible when the ledger arrived.
