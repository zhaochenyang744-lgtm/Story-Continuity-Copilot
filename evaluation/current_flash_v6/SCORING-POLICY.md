# V6 pre-output scoring policy

This is seen-development scoring declared before any V6 Provider output. The 24 inherited story inputs and accepted V3 allowed outcomes remain intact. V6 changes the evidence-role interpretation and model-facing coverage contract; any V5 raw replay is marked offline contract adaptation, never a new model result.

For every raw answer, validate the V6 `claim_verdicts` ledger and Issue schema against that evaluation's exact request. A malformed/absent ledger, missing claim, duplicate, foreign ID, wrong mapping, or failed product validator is a raw structural failure. A repair answer is scored separately against its own repair request. For final product, any status other than `completed` is terminal failure, including empty Issues. A completed run with empty Issues passes only when the case's predeclared allowed outcome includes no issue.

For a confirmed conflict, `relation` is relative to the main story-fact proposition. Machine scoring requires selected cited IDs, complete declared minimum source set, no undeclared extra source, at least one `contradicts` source with local `sufficient`, and no `supports` mislabeled as proof of conflict. The directly incompatible fact may be accompanied by a `context` identity/rule/time premise. The three inherited joint-proof source pairs are: North Glass carrier readiness chapter 4 `context` plus chapter 5 `contradicts`; Orchard boat launch chapter 4 `context` plus chapter 5 `contradicts`; Basalt lens identity chapter 1 `context` plus chapter 2 `contradicts`. In the new lens control L1 is `context` and L2 `contradicts`; in the new capsule control R1 is `context` and R2 `contradicts`. Other inherited confirmed cases retain their declared directly contrary minimum sources; permitted optional context is only a selected, declared source and is still subject to semantic review. This is a source-role scoring policy, not product branching by case ID.

For `insufficient_evidence`, every cited item remains `context/insufficient`, each chain entry `missing_link`, and actions/proposed change are empty. In the new record-attribution controls, the main answer remains about unknown story fact P; attribution unsupported is explained in words, not a separate Issue. Birth order uses timeline; named responsibility/authorization uses relationship; companion location/action category remains individually reviewable as specified by the control matrix. Ordinary compatible new events can have no Issue. A valid `state_change` can be a non-empty conflict-status Issue with `nature=state_change`, supported prior/later evidence and `reviewed_issue` verdict; it is not counted as a confirmed conflict.

The 10 deduplicated control inputs correspond to 12 frozen semantic controls. C01/C02/C05 are one lens business input, not three model samples. Machine scoring only checks declared structural and source-role rules. Whether the quoted source actually establishes an identity, rule, time scope, or contradiction, and whether an auxiliary context is necessary, remains pending independent semantic assessment. No relation rewriting, post-hoc gold change, or selection of a better repair answer is permitted.
