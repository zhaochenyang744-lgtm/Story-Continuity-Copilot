# Chapter 1 Something Older

On 12 August at 10:20, Hettie Drusk lifted a spiral fossil from the shingle at Varnock Scar. "Finally, something older than your cheese," she told Cavor Uldren. He claimed his cheese deserved a proper burial.

# Chapter 2 Lunch Gets a Date

On 12 August at 11:10, Hettie Drusk served the cheese sandwiches at Varnock Scar. Cavor Uldren arranged the spiral fossil beside his plate as an honoured guest. It declined everything, which he called excellent breeding.

# Chapter 3 The Liquid Course

At 12:05 on 12 August, Cavor Uldren believed Hettie's tin held soup. He fetched a spoon and solemnly announced a liquid second course. Hettie drank straight from the tin. His expression suggested that manners had collapsed.

# Chapter 4 A Glove for the Guest

Hettie Drusk wrapped the spiral fossil in her blue glove before placing it in the picnic basket. Cavor offered it a napkin too. "It has managed without one for quite a while," she said. He withdrew the offer, slightly hurt.

# Chapter 5 The Pocket Donation

Cavor Uldren produced a flat shell from his coat pocket. "One for your collection," he said. The pocket also supplied half a biscuit and a bent fork. Hettie accepted the shell; the fork remained his personal archaeological problem.

# Chapter 6 An Entry with Teeth

Hettie Drusk photographed the spiral fossil for the Varnock Scar novelty prize. She sent the picture from the cliff path, where her phone finally found a signal. Cavor asked whether the prize included cutlery.

# Chapter 7 The Descent

On the way down, Cavor Uldren carried the picnic basket on his head so Hettie could tie her shoe. A gull followed him for six steps. "A review committee," he whispered. Hettie suggested that committees usually wanted biscuits too.

# Chapter 8 After the Picnic

At home, Hettie Drusk set the spiral fossil on her mantelpiece beside the flat shell. Cavor Uldren left his bent fork below them. The display looked impressive until she added a card: THREE SURVIVORS OF LUNCH.
