# Chapter 1 A Sandwich Takes Flight

Fenric Soot's left boot had three brass buckles. He fastened them while Oselda Prinn spread the balloon across Dunwex Basin. His lunch sat on top of the maps; the first gust sent a sandwich sailing past the burner.

# Chapter 2 The Last Rope

Oselda Prinn cut the final launch rope while Fenric Soot crouched in the balloon basket. The ground dropped away with surprising politeness. Fenric rose, bumped his head on the burner frame, and decided to admire the view sitting down.

# Chapter 3 A Lighter Breakfast

Oselda Prinn poured sand from a ballast pouch over the basket's side. The balloon climbed above the ridge. Fenric clutched his remaining sandwich in both hands, treating it as a piece of essential equipment.

# Chapter 4 An Arm on the Track

A figure on Dunwex Basin's north track swung one arm in a wide circle. Fenric Soot waved back until his wrist ached. The track curved behind a hill, taking the figure with it. Oselda Prinn leaned towards a gap between two banks of cloud.

# Chapter 5 The Stone Below

Oselda Prinn's map showed a spiral carved into the Dunwex boundary stone. Fenric Soot bent over the map as the stone passed under their basket. He held down a fluttering corner with his boot. The map acquired a sandy heel print beside the spiral.

# Chapter 6 Landing in Rye

Oselda Prinn landed the balloon in Dunwex Basin's rye field. The basket tipped, and Fenric Soot climbed out on his knees. For a moment the rye seemed taller than the sky. He stood, found his legs still worked, and laughed at the basket.

# Chapter 7 The Lunch Inspector

A goat pushed its nose towards Fenric Soot's sandwich. He offered it a crust while Oselda Prinn folded the empty balloon. A farmer arrived with a cart; the goat inspected the cart too, as if every passenger owed it a toll.

# Chapter 8 Home by Cart

Fenric Soot rode home beside the folded balloon, with Oselda Prinn sharing his seat. Over Dunwex Basin, the clouds were breaking into small white scraps. He shook sand from his boot and promised it a quieter morning.
