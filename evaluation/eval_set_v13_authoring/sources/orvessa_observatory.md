# Chapter 1 A Crater Kitchen

Orvessa Observatory clung to a crater on Glezzet. On his last night there, Delsin Rusk unpacked the kettle he had brought from home. Low gravity drew the tea into a trembling ribbon. Yavren Nezric held out a cup, steadying it with both hands.

# Chapter 2 Click

At 22:15 on 8 August, the latch on Tessel Camera was closed. Delsin Rusk tapped the casing with his spoon and heard a tidy click. Outside, dust drifted past the dome as slowly as flour falling through a sieve.

# Chapter 3 The Last Tone

At 22:40 on 8 August, calibration of Palvek Telescope finished. Yavren Nezric heard the closing tone, rolled his shoulders and reached for the kettle. A bead of tea escaped the spout and hovered above his cuff.

# Chapter 4 Small Suns

Delsin Rusk fitted amber filters to the lamps in Orvessa Observatory's reading nook. The softened light reminded him of the room where his father read at dusk. He sat there with Yavren, their cups held close, while the dome turned above them.

# Chapter 5 Across the Glass

Saffrex Nebula occupied twelve pixels across Tessel Camera's display. Delsin Rusk traced the pale patch with a fingertip, then drew it on his cup sleeve. He slipped the sleeve into his pocket. It would travel farther with him than the kettle.

# Chapter 6 Bread in the Dark

Yavren Nezric left his flour jar beside the oven and laid a towel over the table. The kitchen bell rang, and Delsin caught a warm loaf as it rose from the tray. They ate by the oven's red glow, listening to the faint tick of the cooling dome.

# Chapter 7 A Box of Screws

Yavren Nezric sorted the spare dome screws into the violet drawer at Orvessa Observatory. Delsin watched him from the doorway. Such small tasks would continue after the morning shuttle took him away; the drawer's worn handle would still fit Yavren's palm.

# Chapter 8 The Slow Breakfast

Above the dome, Glezzet's small sun lifted from the crater rim. Delsin packed the kettle, then took out his cup for one last swallow. Yavren stayed at the table. The tea had cooled; for a moment, holding it, Delsin could have been anywhere at home.
